# Complete Smart Money Concepts (SMC) Trading Strategy Manual

## Core Philosophy
This strategy strips away lagging indicators and focuses on reading price action through the lens of **structure, liquidity, and institutional order flow**. The goal is not to predict the market, but to let institutional players show their hand, wait for price to rebalance into an area of wholesale value, and execute using lower-timeframe confirmation.

---

## Part 1: The 8-Step Macro-to-Micro Workflow

This is the strict checklist you must follow before taking any trade.

1. **Define Market Structure on the Higher Time Frame (HTF):** Establish the macro trend (e.g., on the 4H chart). Know if the market is printing Higher Highs (uptrend) or Lower Lows (downtrend).
2. **Identify External Highs and Lows:** Locate key swing points where liquidity is swept. You are waiting for a clean break of structure to validate these bounds.
3. **Confirm Direction with Change of Character (CHoCH):** Wait for a structural shift proving the larger players are taking control in the new direction.
4. **Mark Premium and Discount (OTE):** Use a Fibonacci tool on the new confirmed leg. Only sell in a Premium (above 50%) and only buy in a Discount (below 50%).
5. **Define Areas of Interest with FVGs:** Find Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) that align perfectly inside your Premium or Discount zone.
6. **Pre-Plan Targets (Liquidity Pools):** Identify where price is naturally drawn (prior highs, lows, or equal highs/lows). If logical targets don't offer a 3R minimum, discard the setup.
7. **Wait for Lower Time Frame (LTF) Confirmation:** When price taps your HTF FVG, drop to a 15m or 5m chart and wait for a micro-CHoCH to prove momentum is shifting.
8. **Execute and Manage Risk:** Enter on the LTF confirmation. Place your stop loss at the exact structural invalidation point. Ensure a minimum 1:3 Risk-to-Reward (3R).

## Part 2: Defining the Core Mechanics

### Identifying a Valid Change of Character (CHoCH)
A CHoCH is the first structural signal that institutional order flow is shifting. 
* **Uptrend to Downtrend:** Price must break and close below the **last Higher Low** that created the recent Higher High.
* **Downtrend to Uptrend:** Price must break and close above the **last Lower High** that created the recent Lower Low.

**The Fakeout Filter (Wick vs. Body):**
* **Liquidity Sweep (Fakeout):** If price pierces the structural level but leaves a long wick and closes back inside the range, it is a liquidity sweep. Do not trade it as a CHoCH.
* **Valid CHoCH:** Requires a **solid candle body close** past the key structural level, confirming true institutional acceptance of the new price.

### Fair Value Gap (FVG) Mastery
An FVG is a three-candle sequence representing an inefficiency in price delivery.
* **Bullish FVG (Demand):** The gap between the high of Candle 1 and the low of Candle 3 during an aggressive upward move.
* **Bearish FVG (Supply):** The gap between the low of Candle 1 and the high of Candle 3 during an aggressive downward move.

**Qualifying High-Probability FVGs:**
1. **Displacement:** Candle 2 must be large, impulsive, and full-bodied, proving aggressive institutional volume.
2. **Structural Accomplishment:** The impulse move creating the FVG must result in a clear Break of Structure (BOS) or CHoCH.
3. **Premium/Discount Alignment:** Bullish FVGs must be in a Discount (< 50%). Bearish FVGs must be in a Premium (> 50%).
4. **The "Unmitigated" Rule:** Only trade the first test of an FVG. If it has been tapped before, ignore it.
5. **Liquidity Sweep Confluence:** The best FVGs form immediately after a major run on retail stop losses (sweeping an old high/low).

## Part 3: Timeframe Alignment

Never rely on a single timeframe. Use a top-down approach:

* **The Daily and 4-Hour (The Magnet / Context):** Use these to establish daily bias. Unmitigated FVGs here act as powerful magnets for price. Do not execute directly off these zones; simply mark them as your macro Areas of Interest.
* **The 1-Hour (The Bridge):** Excellent for intraday session direction (e.g., London or NY open).
* **The 15-Minute and 5-Minute (The Trigger / Execution):** Drop to these timeframes *only after* price taps your 4H/1H FVG. Look for a LTF CHoCH here, and place your limit order on the newly formed 5m/15m FVG.
* **The 1-Minute (The Danger Zone):** Highly discouraged due to algorithm-driven noise and false CHoCH signals.

## Part 4: Entries, Exits, and R:R Constraints

### 1. The Entry
* Never enter at the exact moment of a CHoCH. 
* Pull your Fibonacci from the origin of the CHoCH to the end of the new leg.
* Place your Limit Order at the edge of the unmitigated FVG located in your Premium/Discount zone.

### 2. The Stop Loss (Invalidation Point)
* **Rule:** Never use a fixed pip amount.
* Place your stop loss safely just beyond the extreme swing point (the top or bottom) that initiated the CHoCH. If price breaches this, your trade thesis is mathematically invalid.

### 3. Risk-to-Reward (The 3R Minimum & The Veto Rule)
* **1R** = The distance from Entry to Stop Loss.
* **Primary Target** = Must be at least **3R** (3x your risk).
* **The Veto Rule:** If the next logical liquidity target only yields 1R or 2R, skip the trade entirely. Never arbitrarily widen your target or tighten your stop to force a 3R ratio.

## Part 5: Trade Management & Scaling Out

The goal is to finance your risk quickly while leaving a runner for macro moves.

### The Breakeven Rule
* Only move your stop loss to breakeven *after* price breaks the next internal structural point in your favor, or after TP1 is hit. Moving it early guarantees being stopped out by routine pullbacks.

### The 3-Tier Take Profit System
1. **TP1: Internal Liquidity (The Risk-Free Level):** 
   * *Target:* The next relevant swing high/low or first opposing FVG.
   * *Action:* Close 30%–50% of the position. Move Stop Loss to Breakeven.
2. **TP2: The Primary Structural Target:**
   * *Target:* A major unmitigated FVG further down the chart or the origin of the impulse move.
   * *Action:* Close another 30%–40% of the position. Trail stop loss behind new structural swings.
3. **TP3: External Liquidity (The Runner):**
   * *Target:* Major external liquidity pools (Previous Day High/Low, Weekly extremes, obvious Equal Highs/Lows).
   * *Action:* Let the remaining 10%–20% run risk-free for massive R:R payouts.

*Strict Management Note:* If price approaches your stop loss before TP1 is hit, do absolutely nothing. Accept the 1R loss if the invalidation point is breached.
